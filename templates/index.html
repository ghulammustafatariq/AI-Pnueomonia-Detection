Our Vision

Developed for the Lahore University of Management Sciences (LUMS), this project explores the transformative potential of deep learning in healthcare. Our core objective was to engineer a highly reliable system for the rapid and accurate detection of pneumonia from chest X-ray images.

By meticulously fine-tuning a pre-trained VGG16 model on a comprehensive public dataset, we've created a specialized classifier that demonstrates the immense power of Transfer Learning. This approach enables us to adapt sophisticated, pre-existing models to nuanced and critical medical tasks, pushing the boundaries of what's possible in diagnostic assistance.

The AI Under the Hood

Delving into the architecture and performance.

VGG16 Architecture

Our model is built upon the robust VGG16 convolutional neural network, initially trained on millions of images from the ImageNet dataset. We employed Transfer Learning, freezing the initial layers to preserve generic feature detection and fine-tuning the later layers to specialize in identifying pneumonia-specific patterns.

Key Components:

  • 13 Convolutional Layers
  • 5 Max-Pooling Layers
  • Custom Classification Head

Dataset & Training

The model was rigorously trained and validated on a comprehensive public dataset, comprising a total of 5,216 chest X-ray images. This was split into 4,173 images for training and 1,043 for validation, ensuring robust learning. Final evaluation was conducted on an entirely unseen test set of 640 images.
